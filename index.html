Family Rhythm - Aile Ritmi

Application Home & Privacy Policy

About: Family Rhythm (Aile Ritmi) is a private family application used only by our household. It reads and manages our family calendar (class schedules, homework deadlines) so parents, the tutor, and our child can see what is happening each day. It is not distributed to anyone else.

Data we handle: Google Calendar events of the family calendar only. No analytics, no third-party sharing, no selling of data.

Who can use it: Only explicitly authorized family members (allowlisted accounts). Access by anyone else is blocked by design.
